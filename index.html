Set which round every Premier League and EFL team was knocked out of last season's Carabao Cup — any of these teams could be a player's Carabao Cup pick. This is shown on the pre-GW1 dashboard.

Correct the long or short display name for any of the 20 current Premier League teams. Every prediction and score is linked internally by team ID and is unaffected by a name change — if the team is anyone's Carabao Cup pick, that pick and its 25/26 elimination-round history are updated automatically too. (To edit last season's finishing position, see the 25/26 PL Stats tab.)

⚠️ Renaming an EFL team rewrites every reference to it — every player's pick, last season's stats, and Carabao Cup history are all updated together automatically, so nothing gets silently orphaned. Use this only to correct a typo or wrong spelling, not to swap one real team for a different one.

Assign each of the 20 Premier League positions to a status. This controls the Champions League / Europa League / Conference League / Relegated badges shown on the 25/26 table and the live 26/27 table — the current-season rule can be changed mid-season if UEFA allocations shift.
